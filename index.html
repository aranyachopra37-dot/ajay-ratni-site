About

The Storyteller

Ajay Ratni is a Mumbai-based actor, director and editor whose work moves fluidly between performance and craft. Originally from Jharkhand, he leads as an actor in the festival-circuit short film Do Raste and has built an independent body of work spanning vertical web series, ad films, scene work and self-tapes. At 22, he brings a raw, instinctive presence to the screen — grounded, contemporary and unafraid of stillness.

What sets him apart is range beyond the frame: Ajay writes, shoots, performs and edits his own films, a genuine one-person creative force shaped by the discipline of independent filmmaking. Fluent in Hindi and English, he moves comfortably across Bollywood, regional and OTT registers — a natural fit for crossover storytelling.

His approach is defined by a commitment to authentic, character-first narratives — the kind of honest, lived-in work that reads on camera. Based in Mumbai and available to travel, he is open to casting for film, series and commercials, alongside directing and editing collaborations.
